The app opens to the Desk without onboarding or sign-in. The interface and number and date formatting are in English. A fresh installation has an empty journal and a bundled catalogue of 15 products.

To review, open the food catalogue from the Desk and select a product. In its dossier, save it, log a choice or add it to Compare. Add a different product to the other comparison slot. Open Archive to view the collection and dated choices; individual choice entries can be deleted. Manual entry is available in the catalogue and requires a product name.

Opening the scanner on a supported device requests camera permission. If scanning is unavailable, enter barcode digits in the catalogue search field. Online searches and barcode lookups use Open Food Facts; product images may also load over the network. Catalogue dossiers link to Open Food Facts.

The Home Screen widget displays the latest choice and counts. Journal data is stored on the device; a summary is shared with the widget. No account, purchase, advertising or analytics flow is implemented.
