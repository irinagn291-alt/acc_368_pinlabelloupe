PinlabelLoupe is a food-label reference for people who want to inspect ingredients and allergens, compare nutrients per 100 g, and keep a personal record of products and choices.

• Search Open Food Facts by name, brand, or barcode, or browse products on this device.
• Scan a barcode or enter a label by hand.
• Inspect a Dossier for ingredients, allergens, and nutrients per 100 g.
• Save a label to your Collection and filter it by name or brand.
• Compare two labels side by side, including B − A.
• Log a choice and maintain Choice history.
• View the collection and log choices on Apple Watch.
• See your latest choice and collection counts in the PinlabelLoupe archive widget.

Check the package for current ingredients and allergen warnings.
