PinlabelLoupe brings food-label information and your personal choices together in one place.

Find products in the bundled catalogue, search Open Food Facts online by name or barcode, scan a barcode, or enter a product manually. Open a dossier to see available ingredients, allergens and nutrition values per 100 g. Missing information is marked as unlisted or shown with a dash.

Save labels to your collection, log dated choices and compare two distinct products side by side. The comparison shows seven nutrient values and the difference between them. Your Desk keeps recent dossiers close at hand, while the Archive holds your collection and choice history.

On Apple Watch, browse your synced collection and send a choice to your iPhone. The Home Screen widget shows your latest choice and journal counts.

Always check the package for current ingredients and allergen warnings. Catalogue data comes from Open Food Facts contributors.
